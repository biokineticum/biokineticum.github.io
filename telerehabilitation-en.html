AI-Powered Telerehabilitation

Next-generation online visits. Advanced real-time analysis of movement and posture without any extra sensors, guaranteeing privacy via the Matrix network.

📷

Joint Kinematics Analysis

During the visit, we utilize an advanced Artificial Intelligence model (YOLOv8) that continuously analyzes your webcam video feed. This allows us to precisely measure key joint mobility and angular velocity without the need for you to wear any special tracking bands or sensors.

⚖️

Live Balance Tracking

In addition to joint movement, our system dynamically calculates your Center of Gravity (COG) and generates a real-time sway map. This provides the specialist with an immediate preview of your postural stability and any asymmetries, enabling targeted therapy.

Privacy & Federation (Matrix)

Your data is protected by the highest standards

Our telerehabilitation takes place via encrypted connections utilizing the open and decentralized Matrix protocol. We connect through a private server fully controlled by us: matrix.biokineticum.com.

What does federation mean? In traditional apps (like WhatsApp or Messenger), all data passes through a central server of a large corporation. In a federated architecture (like Matrix), servers communicate with each other independently, similar to email. Consequently, no unauthorized third party can access the video feed of your therapy. You maintain complete control and sovereignty over your own data.

How to prepare for the visit (Element)

A brief guide on creating an account in a secure messenger

  1. Download the Element app: Visit element.io and install the client on your PC or smartphone.
  2. Create an account: Open the app and click "Create Account". You can sign up on a free public server (e.g., matrix.org) – it is fully interoperable with our system.
  3. Add us to contacts: Once your account is set up, search for or invite our identifier: @dariusz:matrix.biokineticum.com.
  4. Start your visit: At the scheduled time, answer the video call in the Element app. During the conversation, the specialist will analyze your posture with the help of AI and plan your exercises.
