Machine Learning Telerehabilitation

Next-generation online visits. Advanced real-time analysis of movement and posture without any extra sensors. Zero Large Language Models (LLMs), 100% data protection via your preferred encrypted messenger.

📷

Joint Kinematics Analysis

During the visit, we utilize specialized Machine Learning models (such as YOLOv8) that continuously analyze only the kinematic data from your webcam video feed. The system precisely measures joint mobility without transmitting your voice or image to external servers — we do not use any Large Language Models (LLMs), guaranteeing complete analytical security.

⚖️

Live Balance Tracking

In addition to joint movement, our algorithm dynamically calculates your Center of Gravity (COG) and generates a real-time sway map. This provides the specialist with an immediate preview of your postural stability while maintaining total anonymity and local data processing.

Absolute Privacy & Flexible Payments

Your data is your property

Our telerehabilitation and physiotherapy sessions take place via End-to-End Encrypted connections. We respect your convenience and privacy, connecting through the secure video messenger of your choice (such as Signal, Telegram, WhatsApp, Session, FaceTime, Google Meet, or Jitsi).

I value patient privacy to such an extent that we entirely bypass unnecessary intermediaries and traditional restrictions. In addition to standard bank transfers, consultation payments are also accepted in cryptocurrencies (e.g., Bitcoin, Monero, USDT), allowing you to preserve full anonymity and independence at every stage — from communication, through therapy, to final settlement.

How to Prepare for Your e-Visit

Simple steps for a secure online consultation

  1. Choose your messenger: Let us know which video call app you prefer (Signal, Telegram, WhatsApp, FaceTime, Session, or other).
  2. Schedule & Payment: Reach out via email or our contact form to set a convenient appointment and pick your payment method (bank transfer or crypto: BTC, XMR, USDT).
  3. Start your visit: At the scheduled time, answer the video call. You will be guided through a secure diagnostic session (Machine Learning), followed by a discussion of your exercises and personalized rehab plan.
