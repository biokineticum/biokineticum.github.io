Machine Learning Telerehabilitation

Next-generation online visits. Advanced real-time analysis of movement and posture without any extra sensors. Zero Large Language Models (LLMs), 100% medical data protection via the Matrix network.

📷

Joint Kinematics Analysis

During the visit, we utilize specialized Machine Learning models (such as YOLOv8) that continuously analyze only the kinematic data from your webcam video feed. The system precisely measures joint mobility without transmitting your voice or image to external servers — we do not use any Large Language Models (LLMs), guaranteeing complete analytical security.

⚖️

Live Balance Tracking

In addition to joint movement, our algorithm dynamically calculates your Center of Gravity (COG) and generates a real-time sway map. This provides the specialist with an immediate preview of your postural stability while maintaining total anonymity and local data processing.

Absolute Privacy: Matrix & Crypto

Your data is your property

Our telerehabilitation and physiotherapy sessions take place via End-to-End Encrypted connections utilizing the open and decentralized Matrix protocol. We connect through our fully controlled, proprietary server: matrix.biokineticum.com.

Thanks to the federated architecture, nobody besides you and the specialist has access to the therapy's video feed. I value the privacy of my patients to such an extent that we entirely bypass the traditional financial system. In addition to standard bank transfers, consultation payments are also accepted in cryptocurrencies (e.g., Bitcoin, Monero, USDT), which optionally allows us to preserve full anonymity and independence at every stage — from communication, through therapy, to final settlement.

How to prepare for the visit (Element)

A brief guide on creating an account in a secure messenger

  1. Download the Element app: Visit element.io and install the client on your PC or smartphone.
  2. Create an account: Open the app and click "Create Account". You can sign up on a free public server (e.g., matrix.org).
  3. Add us to contacts: Once your account is set up, search for or invite our identifier: @darek:matrix.biokineticum.com.
  4. Start your visit: At the scheduled time, answer the video call. You will be guided through a secure diagnostic session (Machine Learning), followed by a discussion of exercises and crypto settlement.
