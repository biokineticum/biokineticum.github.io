BioKinEdu Platform & Training

Comprehensive solutions and workshops in motion analysis. We offer practical training in kinematic scripting in Python, as well as the setup and operation of inertial measurement unit (IMU) sensor systems. Training sessions can be conducted directly using professional **Noitom** hardware.

🧠 Virtual Clinical Practice

Learning by Doing: PhysioSim Simulator

PhysioSim is custom simulation software designed for physiotherapy students and early-career therapists. It enables safe, virtual training of clinical reasoning. The application presents users with diverse patient cases with various complaints (spine pain, peripheral joint issues, sports injuries). Learn to conduct interviews, interpret imaging/exam results, and form accurate diagnoses without any risk to actual patients.
