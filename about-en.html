Bridging physical culture sciences with the analytical power of Data Science to pioneer innovations in physiotherapy and biomechanics.

Who I Am

I am a dedicated university lecturer and researcher at Jan Długosz University in Częstochowa, Poland. I earned my PhD in Physical Culture Sciences from the University of Szczecin. For over a decade, I have practiced as a licensed physiotherapist, driven by a passion for optimizing patient outcomes and continually advancing scientific knowledge in my field. My professional journey combines academic excellence, extensive clinical experience, and a deep commitment to digital accessibility and Inclusive Design.

My Mission: Physiotherapy Meets Data Science

I firmly believe that integrating Data Science with physical culture and physiotherapy unlocks groundbreaking discoveries and practical innovations. I actively expand my competencies in this domain, holding certifications in research data management and data science fundamentals. I continuously implement these skills in scientific research, inertial motion capture analytics, and everyday clinical practice.
